# Project Demo Video

To record a demo video for this project, you can use standard screen recording tools (like OBS or SimpleScreenRecorder) while running the simulation.

### Recommended Flow for Demo:
1. **Launch Simulation**: Show the Gazebo environment with the iris drone in the warehouse.
2. **Launch SITL**: Show the ArduPilot console and takeoff.
3. **Launch Bridges & SLAM**: Show the ROS 2 logs.
4. **Launch RViz**: Show the real-time map generation as the drone moves.
5. **Autonomous Navigation**: Show the drone exploring and the occupancy grid updating.
